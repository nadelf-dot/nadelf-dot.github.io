About

Design as a cultural & human practice

I'm Nada — a UX and graphic designer studying Information Science with a User Experience Design track at the University of Michigan.

My work spans brand identity, event marketing, social media, and UX design. I'm drawn to design that is culturally thoughtful, visually purposeful, and built around real community needs.

Fluent in English and Arabic, I bring a bilingual, cross-cultural perspective to every project — from global outreach campaigns to intimate community organizations.
